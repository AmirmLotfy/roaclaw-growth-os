# Design decisions

- Keep an Editorial Ledger with ivory surfaces, pine ink and restrained oxide. Use IBM Plex Sans for the workspace, IBM Plex Mono for measurements, and Instrument Serif for the synthetic storefront product name.
- Put date, currency and fixture provenance beside the figures. Inspiration comes from the inspected campaign reporting interfaces in the [research record](RESEARCH_OUTPUT.md).
- Keep source references and approval in neighboring panes at desktop widths. The product review borrows the hierarchy of a selection and an evidence pane identified in the Attio and Dock references, with original layout and tokens.
- Require the Chrome side panel to confirm the active workspace and the exact bookmark action. The reviewed permissions flows support preserving the identity, requested scope and cancellation at the point of approval.
- Stack audit panes and expose navigation through a keyboard-operable button on narrow screens. Surface state in labels and let focus use a visible teal outline.
