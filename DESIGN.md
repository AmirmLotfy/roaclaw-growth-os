# ROACLAW design

ROACLAW leads with the exact difference between a campaign and its landing page. The product site uses a charcoal canvas, paper-green neutrals, lime actions, Archivo Black display text, and a real storefront photograph. An interactive proof panel shows audited evidence and a verified Chrome receipt in their original interfaces.

The work surfaces share the color roles and compact controls. The dashboard retains a balanced source and action layout. The Chrome companion organizes Inspect, Actions, and Research into keyboard-operable views. Motion is limited to the product image, proof-view changes, and real feedback. A reduced-motion preference keeps the full experience available.
