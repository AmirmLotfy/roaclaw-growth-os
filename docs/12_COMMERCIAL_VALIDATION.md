# Post-hackathon commercial validation

## Beachhead segment
Media buyers and 2–15 person agencies, then ecommerce merchants who already run paid ads. These users have recurring cross-account workflows and a reason to pay for time savings, accuracy and approvals. Supporting SMB owners through the same workspace framework is sensible, but do not overbuild SMB-only CRM before interviews.

## Validation hypotheses
- At least 5 practicing media buyers will run Growth Audit on a representative workflow.
- They can point to one correct, actionable issue grounded in evidence, not a vague general recommendation.
- Client context switching and research retrieval remove repeated manual effort.
- They trust approval previews and action receipts enough to use a browser write action.
- They will pay for the connected-account and team features, not just an AI sidebar.

## Interview tasks
Ask users to audit a real ad-to-landing journey, find a previous saved source, explain a metric discrepancy, prepare a change, approve an action, and run a recurring check. Measure completion, errors, comprehension, consent objections and willingness to pay. Compare against their current dashboard/spreadsheet/manual workflows.

## Monetization (not validated)
- Solo: monthly fixed subscription with usage caps.
- Agency: per active client workspace + team seats, approval controls.
- Merchant/SMB: simplified single-business plan.
- Meter high-cost cloud AI/voice usage transparently and set guardrails.
- No ROI guarantees, automatic ad spend fee or pricing claims until tested.

## Product moat candidates to validate
Cross-surface context with user trust, audit provenance, reliable action history, Arabic market operational fit, native browser memory, strong agency collaboration. These are hypotheses, not proven competitive advantages.

## Legal/commercial readiness
Privacy policy and data processing disclosures, Facebook/Google/Shopify app reviews, terms/retention, incident handling, service quotas, subscription support, brand/trademark clearance and stable production availability.
