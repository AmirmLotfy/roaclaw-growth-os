# Product brief — ROACLAW Growth OS

## Position
A client-aware, evidence-first growth operations platform that connects business context, advertising performance, landing-page experience, and approved work. One cloud SaaS plus a Chrome side-panel extension. A credible commercial path is agency subscriptions and owner-operated business plans.

## Primary audience and pain
1. **Solo media buyer / 2-15 person agency**: constant client switching, manual reports, weak ad-to-landing-page context, uncertainty over what was changed, repetitive research.
2. **Ecommerce owner**: ad spending visibility without understanding why conversion suffers; fragmented storefront, ad manager and analytics data.
3. **SMB lead-gen owner**: campaign leads lack quality context; appointments, calls, forms and website changes disconnected from spend.

## Sharp wedge
**Growth Leak Audit** — select a workspace and page/campaign context; extract observable issues locally; combine with authorized or synthetic performance data in cloud; show evidence-ranked hypotheses, proposed actions and approval; execute a safe browser action, then follow up.

**Positioning statement:** "Find the gap between your ads and your results. Inspect it, decide what to fix, and keep a record of the work."

## Product principles
- Accountable > autonomous: a proposal shows what changes, where, who approves, impact range, status and rollback information.
- Evidence > confidence theater: show data source and time, direct quote/screenshot/URL as appropriate, and distinguish fact / inference / missing data.
- Context > chat: client memory is organized by workspace, not one blended chat.
- Private by intent: local-only mode is an explicit hard boundary; no auto cloud fallback.
- Outcome > feature count: flows complete real jobs; no empty marketing screens.
- Accessible internationally: English production-ready; Arabic/RTL on a deliberate roadmap with at least one tested voice demonstration if available.

## Flagship end-to-end demo scenario
Synthetic ecommerce brand **SABLE & STEM** spends $1,200 in the demo week, records 34 orders worth $3,978 (observed ROAS 3.315; goal 3.6). Campaign source creatives promise a sale price of $99; selected product page displays $119; sample destination URL has an intentionally absent `utm_campaign`. These are explicitly *fictional test fixtures* with links to deterministic evidence. Audit identifies and classifies the mismatches; cloud explains implications without asserting causal attribution; creates a corrected UTM URL; user approves saving a linked research bookmark and scheduling tomorrow's follow-up. The action ledger proves success.

## Other demonstration profiles
- **NORTHLINE GROUP** (agency): workspace with two isolated clients, permission-scoped dashboards and approvals.
- **MORROW DENTAL** (SMB): lead campaigns and appointment-booking funnel; no unsupported medical or ad outcome claims.

## Measurable outcomes to test with pilot customers after launch
Time to assemble a client audit, number of errors found before publication, time to retrieve previous research, rate of correct proposed actions, percentage of actions safely approved/executed, and quality of account isolation. Do not make up savings or ROI estimates.

## Explicit non-goals before hackathon
Fully autonomous spend optimization, actual Meta/Google account publishing, bulk mass messaging, scraping private ad platforms, multi-device native apps, enterprise billing, complex BI platform, multi-year historical semantic indexing, and production-ready WhatsApp messaging.

## Commercial positioning (hypotheses to test)
Solo seat / owner workspace; agency shared workspaces and approvals; advanced connector usage and scheduled-monitoring tiers. Pricing and demand are NOT validated by this brief. Test with paying media buyers and merchants before publishing rates.
