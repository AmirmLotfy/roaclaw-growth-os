# ROACLAW Product Requirements Document

## Product and audience
A business-context-aware Growth Operations SaaS plus Chrome extension for media buyers/agencies, ecommerce owners and small lead-generation businesses. Flagship: **Growth Leak Audit**, which uses real Firebase AI Logic hybrid inference, explains evidence, prepares approved actions, and proves actual execution.
